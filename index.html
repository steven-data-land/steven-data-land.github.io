Personal project / Jan 2026

                
Pricing gaps in correlated markets

                
The question

                
Prediction platforms seemed to misprice correlated outcomes within the same event. Gaps like that don't last, so I wanted to test it fast.

                
What I built

                
A conditional probability model for joint outcomes, compared against platform prices, then stress tested with 500 Monte Carlo simulations of 200 events each.

                
                
Result

                
A recurring 3-5 point gap (model 15-17% vs. implied 12%). 98.6% of simulations finished profitable, and 100-150 real-world observations landed in the expected range at about +75 units.

                
PythonConditional probabilityMonte Carlo
